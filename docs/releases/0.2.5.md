## New

- **Create snippets and quicklinks from the launcher.** Type `new snippet` or `new quicklink`, fill in the form, and save with Return (⌘↩ from a snippet's text). The new item works right away; no config file editing needed. (#202)
- **Lower volume while dictating.** While the Dictate Text shortcut is listening, your Mac's audio drops to a fifth of its volume and comes back when you stop. If you change the volume yourself in the meantime, your change stays. Turn it off in Settings → General → Dictation. (#201)

## Changed

- **Dictation keeps your app in front.** The Dictate Text shortcut no longer opens the launcher. A small floating indicator shows that Volant is listening and the words it hears, then the result, while the app you are typing in keeps focus. Escape cancels without switching apps. (#204)

- **Caffeinate keeps the display on and unlocked.** Sessions now stop the screen from dimming, sleeping or locking, like Caffeine.app. To keep only the Mac awake, turn off **Keep display on and unlocked** in Settings → General → Caffeinate, or add `system` to one command, such as `caffeinate 30m system`. (#194)

## Faster

- **AI Chat.** An idle conversation no longer re-reads its whole transcript from the agent helper four times a second. For a long conversation, that cut background memory churn by over 99% (from about 27 MB to 0.1 MB per second). (#195)

## Fixed

- **Shortcut confirmations.** "Saved" and "Removed" under a shortcut now fade after a moment, and clear when the shortcut changes elsewhere, instead of lingering. (#196)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
